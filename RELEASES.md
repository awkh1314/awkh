## Versioning

- `0.x`: preview stage; interfaces, documentation, and hosted service behavior may change.
- Public experience version numbers do not mirror Core/MCP/Agent internal versions.
- A release is marked stable/GA only after the public service and its documented workflows have passed the corresponding acceptance checks.
